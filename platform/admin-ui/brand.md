## Gradients

Not used. Gradients would weaken the minimal, forensic operational direction.

## Tone and voice

Use direct, specific, active language. Prefer statuses, counts, and outcomes over adjectives. One sentence per idea.

Avoid hype, urgency, emojis, and vague effort words such as “easy,” “powerful,” or “seamless.”

Example: “5 sessions authenticated. 2 jobs watching. 0 failed captures.”

## Usage

- Use one blue accent plus semantic success, warning, and destructive colors.
- Keep cards flat with borders rather than decorative shadows.
- Use mono typography for machine identifiers and changing numbers.
- Keep interactions compact but maintain 40 px minimum hit targets.
- Never use `transition: all` or hardcoded component colors.
